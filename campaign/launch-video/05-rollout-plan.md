emails for the coming-soon services.

## Distribution notes

- Nigerian legal-advertising rules (RPC) restrict how law firms solicit — the film's
  educational, product-demo framing helps, but have a licensed colleague review the ads
  and captions before paid promotion runs under the firm's name.
- Seed the film in SME/founder WhatsApp groups and communities (they are the audience and
  they forward things) — the vertical cutdown with burned-in captions is the forwardable unit.
- Pitch tech press (Techpoint Africa, TechCabal, Nairametrics) with the film + "Nigeria's
  first AI-native law firm" angle on launch day; they cover legal-tech firsts.
