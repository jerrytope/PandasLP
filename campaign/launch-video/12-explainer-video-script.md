**Use:** a fast, product-demo-style explainer distinct from the emotional brand film (file 02) —
this one exists to show, step by step, how the product actually works, for viewers who are
already curious but haven't tried it. Cut down for LinkedIn/X feed and Reels/Shorts.

## SECTION 3 — Voiceover Persona & Audio Notes

- **Voice type:** Nigerian-accented, warm, confident, unhurried — reads like a senior lawyer who's on your side, not a hype-reel narrator. Same register as the brand film (file 02), just paced slightly faster to fit 68 seconds of product demo instead of 75 seconds of story.
- **Pacing:** ~150 wpm — a touch quicker than the emotional brand film, since this is demo-paced, not story-paced.
- **BGM:** upbeat modern synth/lo-fi groove (per brief) rather than the brand film's piano-and-drone — this is the one place the more energetic, "tech product demo" sound is the right call, since the job here is momentum through three steps, not emotional weight.
- **Mix rule:** VO always sits on top; music ducks under every line, never fights the captions.
