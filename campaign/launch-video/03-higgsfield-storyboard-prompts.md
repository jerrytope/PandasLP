# 03 — Higgsfield Storyboard & Prompts

Workflow per shot: generate the **start frame** in Higgsfield **Soul** (image), pick the best
take, then animate with **image-to-video** using the motion prompt + camera preset listed.
Generate 2–3 takes per shot; clips are 3–5s, master timeline needs ~14 shots.

**Paste this style block at the end of every Soul prompt** (keeps the film consistent):

> Cinematic still, shot on 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, near-black shadows
> (#060605), single warm amber practical light (#F5A623), volumetric haze, muted palette,
> premium moody realism, Lagos Nigeria, 24fps film look. No text, no watermarks.

**Character block — paste verbatim wherever OBI appears:**

> OBI: a Nigerian man in his mid-30s, dark brown skin, short neat afro fade with light beard,
> calm intelligent eyes, wearing a plain charcoal-grey short-sleeve shirt, in a small furniture
> workshop filled with warm wood, hand tools, wood shavings.

⚠️ Shots 4a–4c and the PDF in 5a are **screen recordings of the real app** — do not generate.

## SCENE 1 — The Promise

**Shot 1a (4s) — Workshop at night**
- *Soul:* Wide shot of OBI sanding a wooden chair at night, one bare amber bulb hanging above
  the workbench, deep shadows around him, sawdust drifting through the light beam. [style block]
- *Motion:* Slow dolly-in toward Obi as sawdust drifts through the amber light; he pauses
  mid-motion and glances toward his phone. — **Preset: Dolly In**

**Shot 1b (4s) — The phone**
- *Soul:* Extreme close-up over OBI's shoulder of a smartphone in his rough carpenter hands,
  screen glowing in the dark showing a WhatsApp chat, his thumb hovering, workshop bokeh
  behind. [style block]
- *Motion:* Subtle handheld micro-drift, focus racks from his eyes reflected in the screen to
  the screen itself. — **Preset: Focus Rack** *(overlay the actual WhatsApp UI in edit —
  don't trust generated text)*

## SCENE 2 — The Wait

**Shot 2a (3s) — The invoice**
- *Soul:* Top-down macro of a paper invoice stamped OVERDUE resting under a glass cup of tea
  on a wooden table, morning side-light, coins nearby, Nigerian naira notes edge of frame.
  [style block]
- *Motion:* Slow crane down and slight rotate over the invoice, steam curling from the tea. —
  **Preset: Crane Down**

**Shot 2b (3s) — Amaka**
- *Soul:* AMAKA: a Nigerian woman in her late 20s, braided hair tied back, rust-orange blouse,
  standing in a bright small commercial kitchen holding her phone to her ear, disappointment
  on her face, trays of small chops behind her. [style block]
- *Motion:* She lowers the phone from her ear and shakes her head slightly, exhaling. —
  **Preset: Static with subtle push**

**Shot 2c (4s) — Lagos dusk**
- *Soul:* Lagos Nigeria skyline at dusk from a high vantage, Third Mainland Bridge traffic
  light trails, warm city lights against a deep blue-black sky, yellow danfo buses blurred

- *Motion:* Time-lapse feel, clouds crawling, traffic streaking, slow lateral drift. —
  **Preset: Timelapse / Lateral**
