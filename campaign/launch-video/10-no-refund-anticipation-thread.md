# 10 — Anticipation Thread: "We Don't Do Refunds. Here's Why That's a Good Thing."

**Use:** pre-launch trust-building thread — post before or the morning of the Space, separate from
the "LIVE THURSDAY" journey graphic thread. Turns a real, unglamorous policy (drafting fees are
non-refundable — see `app/refund-policy/page.tsx`) into the hook itself, then uses it to explain how
the AI intake actually works. Builds anticipation for tonight without revealing the full reveal.

**Grounded in the real policy** — nothing here is invented: the drafting fee is non-refundable once a
document is delivered (same as any law firm), refunds only apply to duplicate charges, a technical
failure that produced no document, or a failed pandas-serves delivery. The reassurance point is real
too: you see the actual watermarked draft *before* you pay anything, so there's no black-box risk.

---

## The thread

**1/**
Something most companies bury in a footer link, we're going to say first: we don't give refunds once
your document is drafted. That's on purpose. Here's why that should make you trust us *more*, not less.

**2/**
It's the same principle every real law firm operates on. You're not paying for a guaranteed outcome —
you're paying for the work of a senior-level legal interview, a properly drafted document, and a
licensed lawyer's review. That work is done the moment it's delivered, whether or not the other side
ever responds.

**3/**
A refund policy that pays out "if you didn't get the result you wanted" isn't generous — it's a sign
nobody's confident in what they handed you in the first place. We'd rather be upfront about this than
pretend otherwise.

**4/**
Here's the part that actually protects you, though: you see the real, watermarked draft of your Letter
of Demand *before you pay a single naira*. Not a sample. Not a template preview. The actual document,
with your actual facts, drafted by the AI interview.

**5/**
That's the whole point of how our agents work — they're built to run the exact intake a senior lawyer
would run: what happened, what proof you have, where the gaps are. WhatsApp messages, invoices,
screenshots — the agent tells you on the spot if something's admissible or missing, before a judge
ever could.

**6/**
So by the time you're deciding whether to pay, you've already seen the actual work. That's a very
different thing from paying upfront and hoping. It's also why refund requests are rare — the AI does
the same interview a lawyer would, then a licensed lawyer reviews it before anything's served.

**7/**
We built this whole thing to be judged on the document you can already see, not on a promise. If
that's the kind of legal help you wish existed the last time someone owed you money — we're about to
show you exactly what it looks like. Tonight, 7PM WAT. 👀
